# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two buyer groups with $1.5K–$10K discretionary signing authority: lab managers / ops leads / junior PIs at wet labs, vet clinics, testing labs, and research orgs; and owner-operators / GMs of independent restaurants and small multi-unit groups. They land here from outreach or referral and need to answer one question fast: "is this person real, and has he built things like my problem?"

## Product Purpose

Portfolio site for DW Tailored Systems — Devin Wilson's solo practice building AI automations and custom tools for labs and restaurants. The site is deliberately a single page: proof of work (project cards) plus one action (book an intro call). Success = a visitor scans the projects and books a call.

## Brand Personality

Plain, technical, unhurried. First-person singular, never "we". The design says "engineer's desk, not agency deck": monospace body, one serif wordmark, hairline borders, flat paper ground. Quiet confidence over persuasion.

## Anti-references

- Agency-style marketing sites: hero funnels, stat rows, testimonial carousels, exit popups, chat widgets.
- SaaS landing-page grammar: gradient text, glass cards, eyebrow labels over every section.
- Forbidden words in public copy: "bespoke," "Systems Architect," "transform," "leverage," "synergy," "unlock," "empower," "innovative," "cutting-edge," "revolutionize."

## Design Principles

1. The work is the pitch — project cards carry the page; everything else stays out of the way.
2. One page, one action — every path ends at "book a call" or email.
3. Committed minimalism — square cards, 1px borders, flat paper; no decoration that isn't the four-color brand strip.
4. Identity-preservation — paper/teal palette and IBM Plex Mono are settled brand; changes restyle within them, not around them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: text ≥4.5:1 (deep teal #037B7E for colored text on paper, never light teal), visible focus rings, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation.
